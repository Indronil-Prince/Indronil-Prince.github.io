ABOUT THE RESEARCH

From understanding learners to building intelligent systems.

My work investigates how machine learning systems can adapt to people and data under practical constraints, including limited student interaction histories and complex simulation-based scientific problems.

This portfolio spans peer-reviewed conference papers, workshop research, posters, a demonstration paper and earlier applied machine learning studies.
