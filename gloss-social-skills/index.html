An LLM-Guided Tutoring System for Social Skills Training

Michael Guevarra, Indronil Bhattacharjee, Srijita Das, Christabel Wayllace, Carrie Demmans Epp, Matthew E. Taylor, Alan Tay

AAAI 2025 · Demonstration Track · February 2025

GLOSS uses large language models to help instructors create branching social scenarios where learners can rehearse communication skills, receive feedback and review their performance.

AAAI publication ↗Read on arXiv ↗Download PDF ↗
Research question

GLOSS: guided practice for real-world interactions

Classroom instruction alone often does not provide enough realistic, individualized practice for skills such as communication and handling challenging social interactions.

Approach

How the work is structured

01

Co-create realistic scenarios

An instructor works with an LLM to design a scenario using templates, prompts or manually scripted exchanges.

02

Practice through dialogue

A student interacts with a conversational simulation that follows the scenario and can generate new branches when needed.

03

Feedback and review

The system provides immediate feedback and supports performance visualization for learners and instructors.

Highlights

What the paper contributes

GLOSSProposed tutoring framework
Instructor + AICollaborative scenario design
Real-timeAdaptive dialogue branching

The AAAI demo describes a flexible LLM-assisted framework for creating and practicing social skills scenarios. It demonstrates system capabilities rather than establishing causal improvements in learner outcomes.
